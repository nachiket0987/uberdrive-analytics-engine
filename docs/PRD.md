# Product Requirement Document (PRD) — UberDrive Analytics Engine

**Project Name:** UberDrive Analytics Engine  
**Project Description:** End-to-end analytics and intelligence engine for Uber ride data built with Python, Pandas, Seaborn, and Streamlit.  
**Author:** Nachiket Gadilohar  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
UberDrive Analytics Engine translates complex ride telemetry, trip durations, surge pricing, and spatial demand patterns into interactive executive dashboards.

---

## 2. Core Features
1. **Trip Telemetry Analytics**: Analyzes booking frequency, distance distribution, and peak hours.
2. **Surge & Revenue Heatmaps**: Interactive Seaborn and Folium maps visualizing rider demand density.
3. **Interactive Streamlit Dashboard**: Filterable UI by date range, trip category, and mileage.
